## Authorized exploratory design brief

This is architectural design exploration. The user explicitly asks for two or three high-fidelity populated alternatives before further approval; production implementation remains gated. Reuse actual Lumen App, store, Composer, ModelPicker, UsageMeter, settings, attachment handling and message actions. New proof-only chrome may call those real controls and expose real computed state, but must never fabricate quota, latency, token counts or provider availability. No source entry point imports the proof files.

Two composition probes, both with the same conversation and synthetic account:

1. **Index — reading desk with an inspectable margin.** Compact vertical workspace index instead of a chat-history-dominated sidebar. The conversation becomes an editorial sequence; a right margin exposes model/route, context estimates and provider readiness. A unified three-tier composer owns attachment/tool context, multiline writing, and route/context/send controls. On phone the margin becomes an explicit context sheet and the composer stays bottom-owned.
2. **Studio — write on the left, review on the right.** Horizontal workspace navigation and a persistent drafting station beside the conversation, making long prompts a first-class workspace rather than a small floating field. The left station contains upper context controls, an expanding input and complete route/usage/send footer. On phone writing expands into a deliberate composing state while the conversation remains independently scrollable and recoverable.

These differ in navigation, screen allocation, composer placement and reading/writing hierarchy. Neither establishes a final logo. Identity exploration uses full wordmark/type/composition systems and must earn approval rather than relying on a generic small mark.
