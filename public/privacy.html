Lumen

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Lumen is a chat app that connects you to AI models from providers such as Google AI Studio (Gemini), OpenRouter, Groq and Cerebras, using API keys you add yourself. This policy explains what Lumen stores, where it goes and how to remove it.

What we store

  • Your Google account basics: your name, email address and profile picture, used to sign you in.
  • Your content: chats, saved memories, projects, settings, scheduled tasks, usage counts, and small file attachments (up to about 600 KB each). These are saved to your account so they appear on all your devices.
  • Your API keys for the AI providers you connect.

This data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Access rules allow only your signed-in account to read or change it. Larger attachments and documents you add to Knowledge stay on your device.

Where your messages go

When you send a message, it goes from your device straight to the AI provider that answers it, using your own key. Some providers' free tiers may use conversations to improve their models; their own privacy policies apply. Lumen's optional privacy shield can hide phone numbers, emails, ID numbers and similar details before a message leaves your device.

Some providers are reached through a small relay we run on Cloudflare Workers. It passes requests through and does not store your messages or keys.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We don't read your chats.

Shared links

If you create a share link for a chat, a copy of that chat's text can be opened by anyone with the link until you turn the link off.

Deleting your data

You can delete your chats and memories in Settings at any time. To remove everything linked to your account, email hebaghoneim8@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

Children

Lumen is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated and the new version is posted on this page.
