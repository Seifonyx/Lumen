4. Evaluate hosting and operational implications.
5. Design a secure implementation.
6. Preserve existing account/privacy behavior.
7. Obtain explicit approval when new persistent infrastructure, sensitive permissions, or costs are required.
8. Implement and test only when safely authorized.

## 6.3 Evidence-based claims
