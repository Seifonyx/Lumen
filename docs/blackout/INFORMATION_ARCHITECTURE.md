## One product, two connected experiences

**AI Workspace:** conversations, Auto/manual/Layouts, Projects, Tools/Study, Compare, Canvas and Library. **Routing Center:** inspect connections and discovered models; edit Layout routing policy and fallback order; understand existing route evidence and usage. Shared identity, account, model registry, settings and policy owners connect both. Switching views must retain the draft and selected route. The prototype adds no gateway, token vault, request logger, new billing source or new provider authentication path.

## What the two alternatives actually change

**Index** allocates desktop space to a compact workspace index, an editorial conversation, a three-tier unified composer and a visible intelligence margin. Its Routing Center uses horizontal sections and a provider ledger beside policy explanations. On phone, primary views use a bottom navigation bar; context and model details use existing modal/sheet owners.

**Studio** allocates desktop space to persistent writing on the left and conversation/review on the right, under horizontal product navigation. Its Routing Center has a section index and grouped provider inventory across the content plane. On phone, Workspace/Routing Center use a top switch; expanded composing takes over the available writing space rather than merely enlarging the bottom field. History and the existing capabilities stay available through the actual chat drawer.

Both prototypes reuse App/store and actual Composer, ModelPicker, UsageMeter, LayoutEditor, ProviderConnections/Settings and RouteWhy. The new surfaces display state from the current catalog/settings/conversation; seeded values are labelled synthetic. Provider status is discovery status, not successful paid access. The manual preview disables external provider requests. Automated tests intercept one deliberate send solely to inspect real Stop behavior.

## Explicitly unfinished

The alternatives are architectural proof, not approved full branding. A final distinctive mark, full motion, optically refined typography, production provider-detail integration (the focused proof is verified below), finished policy-editor layout and complete iPhone keyboard/device acceptance remain. Existing provider editor and usage panels are preserved canonical components; styling/reorganizing them is not evidence that new 9Router capabilities have been implemented. No persistent route history, quota endpoint, gateway, new OAuth or cost reporting is claimed.

The original Aperture/Relay/Fold screenshots were insufficient and are retired. Gate 1 remains unapproved. Do not present the new alternatives as an approved identity solely because their workflow tests pass.
